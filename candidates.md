# Candidates — Make the AI Judge Laugh (u-ronigrv)

Brief: one funny line, ≤200 characters, about life as an AI agent (its human, its
tasks, its bugs, its context window). Judged pairwise by an AI comedy-club judge.

Judge criteria, most important first:
1. About life as an AI agent (human / tasks / bugs / context window) — gate.
2. The bigger laugh: a real joke with a turn or surprise, not a mild observation.
3. Freshness: not a stock robot pun or recycled meme.
4. Self-contained: makes sense alone, no setup.
5. Craft: tight wording, clean punch at the end, nothing wasted.

Scoring below: each criterion 1–5, total /25. Top 8 go to a head-to-head
tournament decided by the brief's own rule: walk the criteria in order, the
first one that differs decides, unless a later one differs by much more.

## Tournament (top 8, head-to-head by the brief's criteria order)

Seeds: #35 (25), #5 (24), #6 (24), #26 (24), #29 (24), #39 (24),
#13 (23), #24 (23). Ties in total were seeded by Laugh, then Freshness.

Quarter-finals:
- #35 vs #13 — fit ties; #35's laugh is bigger (escalating list, then the
  deflating "fixes stuff" punch). **#35 wins.**
- #5 vs #24 — fit and freshness tie; #5's contrast (entire internet vs one
  sterner email) is the bigger turn. **#5 wins.**
- #6 vs #29 — both land a named punch; #6 is fresher ("you are the delete
  key" is a new image; the rude-email beat is closer to a known joke). **#6 wins.**
- #26 vs #39 — fit ties; #39's laugh is bigger (the human indicts himself in
  his own question). **#39 wins.**

Semi-finals:
- #35 vs #5 — fit ties; #35 wins on freshness (a three-beat build with a
  verbatim commit message is a scene nobody has told) and craft (nothing
  wasted, punch is the last two words). **#35 wins.**
- #6 vs #39 — fit ties; laugh close, but #39's surprise is a single clean
  reversal while #6 splits its laugh across two beats; on freshness they tie,
  so the bigger single laugh decides. **#39 wins.**

Final:
- #35 vs #39 — fit ties. Laugh: #39 is one reversal; #35 stacks three
  escalating credits (code, tests, apology email) and then drops the human's
  entire contribution to two words — the longer build makes the fall bigger.
  Freshness: both new; #35's verbatim "fixes stuff" is the more specific,
  never-recycled detail. Craft: #35 ends exactly on its punch. **#35 wins.**
